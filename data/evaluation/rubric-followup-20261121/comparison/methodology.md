Here y is the fraction allocated to WARM recycling after sorting. It is neither a measured physical substitution ratio nor a second process yield. WARM net lifecycle coefficients already incorporate the source model's recycling assumptions and transport. No independent physical substitution sensitivity is identifiable from this engine; none is added.

For mass m in metric tonnes, allocated recycling a = my and residue b = m(1-y):

- Recycling cost: m[c + d1*r + s + (1-y)(d2*r + f) + y(f_rec - p)].
- Landfill cost baseline: m(c + d0*r + f). Incineration cost stays null because f_inc is missing.
- Recycling carbon: m[L + y(R-L)] * 1000/0.90718474. Other carbon: m times its WARM coefficient times the same conversion.
- Savings/differences are landfill minus scenario. Positive means lower scenario cost/carbon within this hypothetical model; negative carbon is a model net credit, not measured removal.
